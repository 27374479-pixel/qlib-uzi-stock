# V5 M07 — recovery-state source readiness

M06 now gives a causal per-dimension repair polarity, but the supplied books still do not say how many dimensions must improve, which dimensions are mandatory, how long improvement must persist, what starting level counts as a bear context, or how conflicting evidence is resolved.

The lower-volume source says bear-market opportunity appears after the decline settles and recovery begins, and it separately asks traders to review advance/decline participation, limit/failed-limit activity, board structure and the treatment of prior strong stocks. That supports the direction of evidence, not a deterministic aggregation rule.

A market-state preregistration therefore still requires, before any strategy return is inspected: exact mandatory dimensions; aggregation rule; persistence rule; starting-state prerequisite; ambiguity/conflict policy; causal decision time; independent non-strategy-return validation target; and lineage binding to M05/M06.
